## What this proved, and what it did not

**Proved:** the schema builds from zero on a real PostgreSQL 17 + pgvector and the app serves. That is the
first of `CC-119`'s checks, and it had never been done before — every prior migration was proven against a
throwaway container whose table was recreated by hand. `CC-119` stays **open**: the `down()` path was not
exercised, and the generated-schema-vs-entities diff was not run.

**Not proved:** that CI is green. It is **not** — see `CC-150` (the `create-mercato-app` package's tests)
and `CC-151` (the integration specs call `/api/customers/people`, a **parked** module, so 12 of 15 shards
fail). Neither blocks the build: the `docker-build` job passed, and the platform above runs.
